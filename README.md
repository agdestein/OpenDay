# CWI Science Day

- What: CWI Science Day
    <https://www.cwi.nl/en/events/opendaycwi/>
- When: Saturday 3 October 2026
- For who: Kids visit the institute, they should have fun and be inspired to do math and computer science. All the groups have their own stands. The Multiscale Dynamics group has an plasma globe that the kids can touch, the quantum sofware group has qbit games where the kid presses two buttons to rotate an arrow, etc.
- Our contribution: Showcase something from the Scientific Computing group
    <https://www.cwi.nl/en/research/scientific-computing/>
- How: A demo with a computer (kids can interact with keyboard, mouse, monitor).
- Goal for this repo: Ideally, some kind of scientific simulation/game where the kids can interact and see the effects on the monitor. Does not have to be faithful to our research, just inspiring.
